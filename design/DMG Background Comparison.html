why PwrSnap's two look different anyway

They are the same hex. What changes is what sits behind them and how thick the strokes are — a lighter surround and heavier glyphs read duller; near-black and thin strokes read hotter. Both blocks below are #ff8a1f, untouched.

PwrSnap is the correct one. Its DMG draws the wordmark in #ff8a1f — the same --accent the in-app wordmark uses, pinned in deviceRGB so the pixels land exactly. PwrAgent's DMG is coded NSColor(calibratedRed: 0.910, 0.353, 0.227), intending #E85A3A, and the calibrated→device conversion pushes it out to #ef714a. So PwrAgent's DMG wordmark is neither its own UI accent nor the app-icon orange — it's a third value that came from a bug, twice over (wrong source colour and wrong colour space).

The claim in docs/solutions/2026-05-31-brand-oranges-and-app-icon.md that this divergence was deliberate — “PwrSnap's DMG is intentionally tighter than PwrAgent's here” — is wrong and should be struck. It documents a bug as a decision.

Fix belongs in PwrAgent/apps/desktop/scripts/generate-dmg-background.swift: accent and arrowShaft → NSColor(deviceRed: 255/255, green: 138/255, blue: 31/255, alpha: 1), then regenerate build/dmg-background.png. That lands both DMGs on #ff8a1f and nothing else moves.
