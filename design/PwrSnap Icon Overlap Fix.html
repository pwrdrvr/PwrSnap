The mark is a stack, not a blend

The three tiers were painted back → mid → front with plain alpha compositing. Correct Porter-Duff, wrong mark: the 30% back rect shows through the 55% mid rect, so every crossing lights up as a denser, more saturated patch. Fixed by knocking each tier out wherever a tier in front of it covers — front and mid are immutable in colour and opacity anywhere they're seen, back sits behind.

#a5552c is mid-over-back-over-tile — 0.55×accent composited onto 0.3×accent. It is brighter than the mid tier's own #884725 and passes a “saturated orange” filter the real mid tier fails, which is why the crossings read as a lighter wash sitting on the tile. Removing it is the whole visual delta; the ramp underneath never moved.

what changed
  • generate-app-icon.swift — mark section rewritten. Rects become CGPaths; each tier is stroked inside a clip built from copy(strokingWithWidth:) outlines of the tiers in front of it (bounds + ring, even-odd, intersected sequentially). Colours moved off NSColor onto CGContext.setStrokeColor(red:…), which is DeviceRGB — same #e8743a, no calibrated→device drift risk.
  • generate-tray-icon.mjs — two userSpaceOnUse luminance masks (ps-behind-front, ps-behind-mid-front) cut the front/mid stroke bands out of the tiers behind. Geometry, stroke width and both variants unchanged.
  • Nothing else moves. Tier alphas stay 1.0 / 0.55 / 0.3, offsets stay ±64 / ±80, the gradient tile and the two-orange split are untouched. The in-app SVG mark (AppIcons.jsx / FoMark) already uses three opaque tints and never had this bug.
